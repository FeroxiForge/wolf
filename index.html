Welcome to the Wheel Of Lost Fortune, a.k.a. the WOLF. The WOLF hungers for players' hard-earned scrap. Feed it well, prodigals of Rust.

You begin with 12 scrap. Choose one or more numbers and place your bets. Your cumulative stake cannot exceed the total amount of scrap you currently have. You must place at least one bet before you can spin the wheel.

If the wheel lands on a number you backed, your winning bet is returned together with winnings equal to that number multiplied by your stake.

Use 🔼 and 🔽 to adjust stakes, ❌ to clear an individual stake, or RESET ALL to clear all current bets. Your Scrap balance is stored locally in this browser.

WOLF uses imaginary scrap only. There is no real money, no worldly value, no prizes, and absolutely no reason to take any of this seriously.
